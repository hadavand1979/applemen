Our Story

MORE THAN A BAR

Applemen Sports Bar is Inwood's go-to destination for great food, cold drinks, and good times. Fully renovated with an upscale modern look, we've got everything you need for a perfect outing — whether you're catching the game or hanging out with friends.

From our massive outdoor deck and inside-outside bar to pickleball courts and live music events, there's always something happening at Applemen, and kids can stay and have fun until 9pm.
